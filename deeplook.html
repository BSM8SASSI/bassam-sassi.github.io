DeepLook ROV

A 6-Degree-of-Freedom Underwater Remotely Operated Vehicle
Designed, simulated, and built from scratch in Medenine, Tunisia.

📸 View Gallery 📋 Specifications
Project Overview

From Concept to Reality

DeepLook evolved from a 3-thruster simulation to a fully operational 6-DOF prototype, combining mechanical design, embedded electronics, and control systems.

🎯

Objective

Design and build a low-cost, high-performance underwater ROV capable of 6-DOF movement for marine exploration, inspection, and research applications in Tunisian coastal waters.

⏱️

Timeline

Jan 2026 – Jun 2026
6 months from concept to working prototype. Supervised by Madame Hazar at ISET Djerba.

🏆

Result

Mention Très Bien — 18.2/20
Ranked 3rd in the department. Fully operational prototype with validated waterproofing and wireless control.

Technical Details

Specifications & Architecture

🔧 Mechanical Design

📐

Chassis Design

Hydrodynamic chassis designed in SOLIDWORKS with optimized flow characteristics. Central control pod, battery cradle, and 6-DOF thruster mounts.

SOLIDWORKS CFD
🌀

Thruster Nozzles

Custom-designed and 3D-printed thruster nozzles for optimal water flow. 6 motors arranged for full 6-DOF control: 4 vertical + 2 horizontal.

DFAM 3D Printing
🌊

CFD Analysis

Flow Simulation analysis: velocity vector mapping, drag coefficient optimization, and acoustic signature reduction for stealth operation.

Flow Sim Analysis

⚡ Electronics & Control

🧠

Control Architecture

Arduino Nano — Real-time motor control & sensor reading
Raspberry Pi 4 — High-level processing, video streaming, data logging
Serial communication between boards for coordinated operation.

Arduino Raspberry Pi
🔌

Power System

3× 30A ESCs (Electronic Speed Controllers) for brushless motors. Battery management system with voltage monitoring and thermal protection.

ESC Power Mgmt
📡

Communication

Serial communication protocol for motor commands and sensor feedback. PS4 controller integration via Bluetooth for wireless operation.

Serial Bluetooth

💻 Software Stack

🐍

Python (Raspberry Pi)

High-level control logic, sensor data processing, video streaming, and data logging. Integration with OpenCV for computer vision capabilities.

Python OpenCV
🔧

C/C++ (Arduino)

Real-time motor control with PID algorithms. PWM signal generation for precise thruster speed and direction control.

C/C++ PID
🎮

PS4 Controller

Custom mapping of PS4 controller inputs to ROV movements. Low-latency response for intuitive piloting experience.

HID Wireless

Development Journey

From 3 to 6 Thrusters

January 2026
Project Initiation
Defined project scope: design a low-cost underwater ROV for Tunisian waters. Initial research on thruster configurations and waterproofing methods.
February 2026
3-Thruster Simulation
First CAD model with 3-thruster configuration. Basic hydrodynamic analysis and initial flow simulation in SOLIDWORKS.
March 2026
Design Evolution to 6-DOF
Upgraded to 6-thruster configuration for full 6-DOF control. Redesigned chassis, thruster mounts, and control pod. DFAM optimization for 3D printing.
April 2026
Prototype Assembly
3D printing of all components, electronics integration, wiring, and initial bench testing. ESC calibration and motor synchronization.
May 2026
Testing & Validation
Waterproofing tests, hydrostatic pressure validation, wireless control testing, and performance optimization. PS4 controller integration.
June 2026
PFE Defense — 18.2/20
Final presentation and defense. Mention Très Bien with departmental ranking of 3rd place. Fully operational prototype demonstrated.
