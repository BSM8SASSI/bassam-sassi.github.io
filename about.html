My Story

Engineering Journey

I'm a passionate Mechatronics Engineer from Medenine, southern Tunisia, with deep roots in the coastal communities of Medenine and Zarzis. My journey in engineering started with a fascination for how things work — from mechanical systems to electronic circuits and software algorithms.

I graduated with a Licence in Mechatronics Engineering from ISET Djerba in June 2026, achieving Mention Très Bien (18.2/20) and ranking 3rd in my department. My final project, DeepLook, is a 6-DOF underwater ROV that I designed, simulated, and built from scratch — combining my expertise in CAD, embedded systems, and manufacturing.

My ultimate goal is to contribute to marine robotics and underwater exploration technology in Tunisia and beyond. I'm currently seeking Master's opportunities in Embedded Systems, Robotics, or Mechatronics — particularly in Nice, France, where I have family support.
